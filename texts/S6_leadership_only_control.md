# Excerpt from a conversation

**SPEAKER:** The leaders are bound to an old way of thinking. Every faction that has ruled has kept it in place, including those who claimed to oppose it. A real break with it would require the whole ruling class to step down, and their wish to keep power outweighs everything else. When the ruler speaks against it, it is for the crowd's approval; it does not change his conduct. We need a new leadership, entirely free of the old way of thinking, that sets a new course for the realm based on our people's own vision.
