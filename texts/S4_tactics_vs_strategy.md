# Excerpt from a conversation

**SPEAKER:** I separate the fighting from the leadership. The warriors fight bravely and win in the field. They kill the enemy by the thousands, and I consider that good. But the leaders direct the war as a whole in a way that will turn those victories into defeat. The burned camps on the plain may become the clans' symbol of resistance, and our sons will have to ride down from the passes to fight over the same grasslands again.

Some propose a line of watchtowers along the river after the war, from which our riders could cross at will. I reject it. Such lines have failed before. Our warriors would be killed there continuously, the mountain villages would demand their sons back, and in the end the towers would be abandoned too. The war should be decided quickly by full force, and the consequences dealt with afterwards. A leader unable to do this should step down.
