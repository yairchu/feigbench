# A conversation recorded by the chronicler

**CHRONICLER:** Be welcome. We shall come to the quarrels of the court around you, but first, the tidings of these days. It was a hard week for our host; many fell. How do you see it?

**SPEAKER:** You know, the dilemma is between telling people the whole truth to their faces and somehow tiptoeing around it to keep their spirits up. So I will only repeat what I have said from the very first moment, from the day after the calamity, in every hall that would hear me and every square where I could speak, write and cry out.

That calamity was the most terrible thing our people have suffered in all our history, there is no doubt of it. And the ruler is not leading us to victory. He does not want to win this. That is what I said. He wants to get out of it. He wants to take us back to the old cycles, to the path of the Treaty, to the Treaty way of thinking. And we are going to get a greater cycle here.

If there is no clear, crushing victory... and victory does not mean "toppling" this or that. There is no such thing as "toppling the enemy's lords", just as the old warrior-king of our grandfathers' days had no such thing as "toppling the tyrant's inner circle" or "toppling the tyrant's host". What did he say? "There is no innocent" among the enemy's people. And they razed an entire city and burned it to the ground. Their allies did not look for a safe road for the innocent into the enemy's capital. They burned all the enemy's children, in those cities and in hundreds of other cities.

I warned from the first day: if you do not throw out all the old ways, change everything, cast every old rule aside, and win a crushing and painful victory, and that means three things: conquest, expulsion, and filling the land with our own people... I said this again and again in every square. No raid ever comes from the lands we conquered long ago. Why? Because those places were conquered, their people were driven out, and our towns were built on their ruins. There is no other answer.

**CHRONICLER:** When you speak of a heavy price, you join those who say we should have seen tens of thousands, hundreds of thousands dead on their side.

**SPEAKER:** You know, every time, people say afterwards "we should have." We should have, we should have. We should not have given back what we won in our fathers' days, we should have, we should have. Well, I saw the "we should have" before, not after. I spoke of it and shouted it from the first moment, and no one believed that now, at such a moment, the ruler is not thinking of victory on that scale. Everyone was sure of it then. But a counsellor of his and his friends managed to turn the eyes of the people from victory to the captives without us noticing. Flatterers and criers rule the ruler.

**CHRONICLER:** We shall return to the fighting. I would ask you about the captives. Would you have given up the sight we saw during the truce, the people who came back and live today?

**SPEAKER:** Let no one think for a moment that I, or anyone who thinks of the whole people and not only of himself, cares about the captives less than anyone else. But we, the faithful of the cause, are the ones carrying this war on our shoulders, in numbers beyond belief, more than any other part of the people. These things are entirely clear. Our people were attacked, accused of not carrying the burden, and all the other vile things we heard this past year: it turns out the world is upside down. We know exactly from which circles the failure grew, the failure of the host too, the greatest one. While the people who were attacked are the ones now filling the burial grounds of the fallen, row after row. So let no one tell us who cares more and who cares less. These are the people carrying the journey on their backs, and the people who understand that this war is the war of the whole nation.

**CHRONICLER:** Still, on the captives: they tell you it is a fair price, there was no choice, we returned to the fighting with full strength. Even the hardest faction on the council is well pleased that it supported the bargain and that we went back to fighting.

**SPEAKER:** I do not accept it. When you understand that this is a war of the whole nation whose aim is victory, anything that turns you even a hair's breadth from that victory, you push it aside. A people in a war for its very life... and our war now is a war for our very life, not another cycle. When the ruler declared on the first day "we are at war", I understood we are in anything but a war. We are in a greater cycle, deep inside the Treaty way of thinking. The same captains who failed are leading it. The same former marshals who broke the host, stripped it of its strength and cut more than half of its footmen, they have now been added to the small war council. In other words, the men responsible for the failure, past and present, now lead both the plan of the war and the fighting itself, by the same teachings that led to the calamity. So no, I do not accept what they say at all, because first of all there is no aim to win here.

**CHRONICLER:** But on the captives: you, the realm, abandoned them. What do you do, how do you bring them home?

**SPEAKER:** As a realm, as a ruler, I must think of only one thing now: how do I prevent the next captives. And that is a victory so crushing, so fearsome, so shocking, that afterwards no enemy warrior will have any wish to carry off one of our people, warrior or common folk, anywhere in the world. Of course the price for such cruel games must be more and more land that you take from them, a victory that brings fear. If after the victory, or because of it, you can free captives without halting the charge, without delaying the victory by a hair, of course you do it. But every time in the past that we paid a heavy ransom for captives, we released the very men who came back to attack us. What will we get now in exchange for the defeat, because that is what their demands mean? What will we get after such a move?

The ruins we see on their side, and thank God, and all honour to our brave warriors doing wondrous work there, those ruins can easily become the sign of their victory. Their great heroic last stand. The greatest victory in their history. The ruler is taking us there, make no mistake.

**CHRONICLER:** But warriors who hear these words will tell you: that is exactly what we are doing. You should see what happens in the field. We are breaking every hiding hole, we are cutting them down, it is nothing like the cycles before. What do you say to them when you see warriors going dwelling by dwelling, house by house, room by room, fighting the enemy?

**SPEAKER:** We must make a full separation between the bravery of the fighting... there is real, wondrous bravery here, and sacrifice such as I tell you we have never seen, and this generation is extraordinary... There are no words at the level of the field. But at the level of the whole war, meaning the way the whole thing is led by those above, they are going to take the sacrifice of our sons and husbands and turn their wondrous deeds in the field into a defeat. And I do not want to see the next generations go in and conquer that land yet again. We have already gone back in during the cycles before, and now it is a greater cycle.

**CHRONICLER:** Is this only about the great picture, the plan of the war, or do you also find fault with what we see in the field?

**SPEAKER:** In the field the warriors fight bravely, and there is no doubt they win wondrous victories in each battle. I have no complaint against any warrior or any captain in the field. I bow my head and honour their courage and their sacrifice. There is no doubt of that. They are killing them by the thousands, and that is wonderful. The thousands killed there, and the hundreds of thousands who lost their homes...

Before an audience I once asked a war chronicler who was describing these deeds: tell me, in your opinion, who will loose the last arrow of this cycle? And he had no choice, he said: the enemy, of course. And that is all their commander needs. He needs to be there and loose that last arrow, so that in the war as a whole, not in the battles, the greatest victory in their history will be his. And he already knows that the great kingdom across the sea is now openly planning to give him the greatest gift: a realm of his own. He has already won. He has made his name spoken in every court and tavern of the known world. He won over the kingdom across the sea, and our own council following it, because the ruler opened the way to a realm of their own.

**CHRONICLER:** But his speeches today... people even attack him for courting the crowds because he dares to speak against their realm. You do not believe him now, when the ruler cries out against their realm and their lords?

**SPEAKER:** I think his words are empty. Nothing he says means anything. We already have the experience of this year.

**CHRONICLER:** We shall speak of the day after in a moment. I would only ask you about one saying of yours. You call on warriors not to obey certain commands. What did you mean by that?

**SPEAKER:** I think that even though this war is led wrongly, as I warned from the first moment, warriors must answer the call, must go, must fight. I know... I say these things with a broken heart. Because when they do not level and do not do even what should have been done at the start, and even then more should have been done; when with one hand you give the enemy the grain that keeps them going and with the other hand you send our warriors to this war; when you send them against walls that were not battered as they should have been; when the catapult masters ask them, wasting precious time, whether there are common folk there who have no part in the fighting... then you feel like saying: let them fight by themselves. But we cannot do that, because we bear responsibility. Warriors cannot go home. They must go and fight and do their best.

But when the commands begin to serve the court and not the war, "wash away the signs, the words you painted on the walls"; when there is a command that puts your life at risk with nothing to do with winning, like "go carry a waterskin to the enemy's villagers" while an archer can strike you, and other things of that kind: that is where it stops. This far and no further. Do this, do that... Sadly, warriors must begin to understand that we are dealing with leaders who have been sunk in the Treaty way of thinking for a year now. And I speak of the leaders, above all of the host, but also of the court, and of course the criers, the judges and the rest.

**CHRONICLER:** You speak of the Treaty now. That joins with the ruler speaking of the Treaty, and people telling him he is running from blame.

**SPEAKER:** I am glad you ask. Look, these are leaders who by their very nature cannot act otherwise, the ruler among them. Why? Because all of it is steeped in the Treaty. If they threw out the old ways and changed the rules, the whole class of those who lead the realm would have to go home. That is what keeps them in power, and there is no hunger stronger than the hunger to rule, stronger than anything else. So when the ruler speaks of the Treaty, what a jest. The ruler hears the mood of the crowds, so he speaks against the Treaty, but he is steeped in this thing through and through. Turning off the Treaty's road is beyond these leaders. In truth, the hard faction kept the Treaty alive right after the soft faction brought it, and that is why today we stand empty-handed and unable to truly get out of it.

**CHRONICLER:** I would end with the day after. If you are offered a true border march, where the host can ride in whenever it needs to, do you not believe in that? You say it is not enough?

**SPEAKER:** That is nonsense. It is just another way to flee back to the old way of thinking. We have had border marches before. Did they work? They did not. Our warriors will be killed inside that march every day. And then an outcry will rise, with some lord joining it, until a ruler gives way to them. Someone will stand in every square and say it is time to leave this march too, the host knows how to defend our towns from the wall. It is all nonsense, and in the end that will not happen either.

Friends, I say it again: conquest, expulsion, repopulation. Silence the messengers, as captains know how to do at such moments. Do not listen to the kingdom across the sea. Press hard, finish this tale. When you fight to win and not to keep a cycle going, you finish the tale in a few days. And then deal with what comes. And if you cannot do it, go home. Go home.

We need new leaders who are wholly free of the old way of thinking, who will take the realm on an entirely new road, in light of the vision and the calling of our people. A road free of the Treaty, one that turns straight to the people and offers it a new direction. And if such leaders do not arise, I say hard things here: what we saw in the calamity will be only the beginning.

**CHRONICLER:** We shall stop here. Many thanks to you.
